Currencies

Your base currency (set above) is used for all totals, balances and reports. Add other currencies you exchange money in — the rate is used only to suggest an amount when recording an exchange; the actual amount exchanged is always what you enter at the time.
Sale, Purchase, Expenses and Payments show both currencies at once, e.g. "500.00 USD · ≈650,000.00 SSP" — this switches on automatically once you've added a currency above. Pick "Off" here if you only want to see the base currency.

Tax rates

Define tax rates (e.g. VAT, GST) once, then apply one to a Sale Invoice, Mobile POS sale or Purchase Bill — the amount is calculated on the item subtotal and added to the total.

Shops

Used as delivery destinations for stock sent to your own shops.

Projects

Used as delivery destinations for stock sent to a site / project.

Companies

Keep separate books for each business. Switching companies swaps out all data — items, sales, parties, reports — for the one you pick. Logins are shared across every company.

Users & roles

Add a login for each person on your team. Seller can make sales and view items only. Cashier only handles Payment-In, Payment-Out and Expenses. Store Keeper sees Items (add, adjust stock, deliveries). Viewer only sees the item quantity and item reports, view & print only. Admin sees everything, including this screen.

Activity log

Every sale, purchase, payment, expense and stock change made by Seller, Cashier and Store Keeper shows up here so Admin can see who did what.
